Meet your boombox

01 / Build a groove

Choose a genre to load a starting rhythm. Tap any of the 16 steps across six voices. Root and scale tune the synth parts; swing loosens the pulse. Variation reshapes patterns, voices and effects within your genre, keeping master volume. Settings lets you keep an exact sound across visits.

02 / Blend your own audio

Load two local tracks in Remix. Adjust rate, EQ and loop points, then blend with the crossfader. Reverse restarts playback in the opposite direction. Nothing is uploaded; browser memory and supported codecs limit what can be decoded.

03 / Play with code

Load an example, edit its notes and rhythm, then Run code. Use the Syntax field guide for musical commands, synths and samples. Export .rb to keep your source or explore it further in Sonic Pi.

Keys & listening

Space toggles the groovebox when you are not editing or using a control. Esc closes a sheet. Start at a low volume. Stop silences the groovebox, both decks and live code.

Local by design

Projects persist in browser storage when available. Export JSON for a backup. Files and recordings stay on your device; closing or reloading releases loaded tracks. WAV rendering exports the pattern, not your uploaded tracks.
